Voice & rules

✓ Do

  • Talk like the seller who remembers your order: short, warm, a bit cheeky.
  • Keep the board white with ink lines; red and blue only as squiggles and highlights.
  • Draw people and food as single-weight line doodles.
  • Write prices short: 25, not Rp 25.000,00.

✕ Don't

  • Fill whole surfaces with red or blue.
  • Use photos with heavy filters or stock food shots.
  • Shout with “PALING ENAK SE-INDONESIA!!!”.
  • Redraw the wordmark in another typeface.
