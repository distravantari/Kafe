Brand guidelines · v1.0

Warm, cozy,
a little sleepy.

How Kafe looks, sounds and feels. Dark warm colours for a cozy room, chunky rounded type, and a hand-drawn mascot that reminds everyone to slow down.

01

Logo

The Kafe wordmark is set in Fredoka Bold with tight tracking, finished with a coffee bean that sits where a full stop would be. It's the only logo — don't redraw it in another typeface.

02

Kopi, the mascot

Kopi is a sleepy paper cup drawn in a single-weight rounded line. Always one colour, always line-only (fill matches the background). Use on packaging, merch, social and empty states — never next to the logo at the same size.

03

Colour

Brick does the heavy lifting — it's the wall, the cup, the menu. Cream and Crema are the "ink" on top of it. Maroon is for depth; the earthy neutrals support body copy and print details. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

05

Voice

We talk like the barista who remembers your order: warm, short, a little playful. Never salesy, never rushed.

Warm

"Pull up a chair, the kettle's always on."

Unhurried

"Brewed slow, served warm."

Playful

"Sit, sip, nap if you need to. We won't tell."

✓ Do

  • Lead with feeling, then the facts
  • Use short sentences and plain words
  • Let Kopi crack the jokes
  • Keep generous rounded corners (22–36px) and pill buttons

✕ Don't

  • Shout with "BEST COFFEE EVER!!!"
  • Use cold blues, greys or neon
  • Use photos with harsh flash or cool white balance
  • Put the mascot and logo side by side at equal size
06

Applications

Packaging lives on white or cream with Brick graphics; in-store surfaces (menus, signage, cups) flip to full Brick with Cream ink.
