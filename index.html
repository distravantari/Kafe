Our story

A little room for slow mornings.

Kafe started with one idea: coffee tastes better when nobody's rushing you. We roast in small batches, dial in every shot by hand, and keep the music low enough to hear your friends.

Kopi — the sleepy cup on our walls — is a reminder to slow down. Sit, sip, nap if you need to. We won't tell.
